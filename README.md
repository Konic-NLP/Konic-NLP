# Hi there, I'm Sijia Ge 👋

You can also call me **Konic**.

I am a Computer Science PhD student at the **University of Cincinnati**. My research interests include **AI alignment, interpretability, commonsense reasoning, and computational semantics**.

Previously, I worked as an ML/AI Engineer at [Rippey.AI](https://rippey.ai/), a startup specializing in logistics and supply chain automation. I received my master's degree from the **University of Colorado Boulder**.

## Research & Projects 🔍

I am broadly interested in:

* Natural Language Processing
* Computational Linguistics
* Machine Learning and Deep Learning
* Large Language Models
* Web Development

I developed [UMR-Writer](https://verbs.colorado.edu/UMRWriter/), a web-based annotation tool for **Uniform Meaning Representation (UMR)**.
